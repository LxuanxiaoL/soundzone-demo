This product includes data developed at the Audio Lab, University of York,
by Cal Armstrong, Lewis Thresh and Gavin Kearney as part of the SADIE Project.

CHANGES: Official 48 kHz RAW stereo PCM samples were converted to a float32 NPZ
with coordinates parsed from filenames. No EQ, gain normalization or time shift
was applied. All 1555 files are retained; six north-pole measurements represent
one geometric direction, giving the documented 1550 unique directions.
This is a different measurement campaign from SADIE II D2; it is not a recovery
or synthetic reconstruction of the SADIE II unequalized response.
The official archive and member hashes preserve the unmodified source files.
